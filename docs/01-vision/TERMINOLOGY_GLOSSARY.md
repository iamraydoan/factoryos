# Terminology Glossary

## Domain Terms

* **BOM (Bill of Materials):** Structured list of raw materials, sub-assemblies, and components required to manufacture a finished product.
* **CMMS (Computerized Maintenance Management System):** Domain service managing equipment health, preventive maintenance schedules, and work orders.
* **Digital Twin:** Real-time software representation of physical factory entities (machines, stations, lines, tools, operators).
* **Lot / Batch:** A specific quantity of material or serialized components produced under uniform conditions.
* **MES (Manufacturing Execution System):** Traditional category of shop-floor tracking and control software.
* **NCR (Non-Conformance Report):** Formal quality documentation created when a component or material fails quality specifications.
* **OEE (Overall Equipment Effectiveness):** Industry benchmark metric calculated as `Availability × Performance × Quality`.
* **OPC-UA / MQTT / Modbus:** Standard industrial operational technology (OT) protocols used for machine sensor data communication.
* **Routing:** The defined sequence of operations and stations required to complete a work order.
* **SPC (Statistical Process Control):** Method of quality control using statistical metrics to monitor process capability and stability.
* **Station / Work Center:** A specific physical location on a line where an operator or machine performs manufacturing steps.
* **Traveler:** Physical paper document traditionally attached to a batch of parts detailing routing steps and sign-offs.
* **Work Order:** Authorized request to manufacture a specified quantity of goods according to a routing and BOM.
